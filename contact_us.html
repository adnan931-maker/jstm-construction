Client Procurement FAQ

Essential Questions Before Tendering

Clarity before engagement. Here is how JSTM Construction operates on prime London residential projects and joint-venture frameworks.

speed

How quickly do you turn around tenders?

For fully specified architectural drawing sets and Schedules of Work, our commercial department provides line-item BoQ pricing within 5 to 10 working days. For preliminary feasibility appraisals, we can provide cost-per-square-meter guidance within 48 hours.

handshake

Do you work alongside appointed RIBA architects?

Yes. Over 70% of our turnover is realized in direct joint venture with London’s leading architectural practices and interior designers. We act as principal main contractor, respecting design integrity while contributing constructive value engineering.

account_balance_wallet

How are cost overruns prevented?

We contract under standard JCT Minor Works or Standard Building contracts with fixed-price commitments for clearly scoped packages. No variation is undertaken without prior written sign-off by the client or contract administrator.

policy

Do you handle Party Wall notices & council licenses?

Our in-house project managers coordinate directly with your appointed Party Wall surveyors, arrange scaffolding and skip highway licenses with Westminster, Kensington & Chelsea, or Camden councils, and ensure complete statutory compliance.

help_outline

Have a complex listed building enquiry?

Speak directly with our Historic Environment & Conservation consultant.
